Privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy describes how Ellis Software handles information when you visit this website or contact me. This site is a portfolio and does not currently use an account system, analytics service, or on-site data collection form.

Information you choose to share

If you use the contact link or form, your email application opens with the name, email address, and project details you entered. The message is sent only if you choose to send it. I use information you send to respond to your inquiry and discuss potential work. The website itself does not submit or store that form data.

Website services and technical information

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, icons from the Lucide library, and some images or icons from third-party content delivery networks. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details when your browser requests those resources. The website host may also process routine technical logs to deliver and protect the site. These services have their own privacy policies and practices.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not intentionally set tracking cookies or use analytics. The website host or third-party services used to deliver site resources may use necessary technologies as described in their own policies.

Retention and your choices

If you contact me, I keep your message only as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage our conversation, or meet applicable legal obligations. You may ask me to update or delete information you have sent by emailing techellissoftware@gmail.com.

Children’s privacy

This portfolio is not directed to children, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
